VIDEOBOOST · VERSION 1.0.0

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Publisher: Achraf Mesnaoui.

Purpose

VideoBoost lets users control playback speed, picture settings and audio volume on the selected browser tab.

Data access and processing

When the user opens the extension, it checks the selected tab for accessible video elements and reads the playback rate and visual settings needed for playback controls. When requested, it changes these settings and can dim the surrounding page. Audio boost uses an audio stream from the selected tab only after the user activates it. The stream is processed in memory on the device and played back locally. It is not recorded or uploaded. The boost can be stopped from the extension and is stopped when the tab closes or navigates.

Storage and retention

The selected interface language is saved locally in Chrome extension storage. Video settings and audio processing state are held temporarily in memory. The extension does not retain audio recordings, browsing history or page content. Uninstalling the extension removes its local settings.

Sharing and security

Version 1.0.0 does not send user data to the publisher, third parties or external services. It does not sell data, use analytics or advertising trackers, or require an account or payment. Data is used only for the disclosed video and audio controls.

Permissions and user choices

activeTab and scripting allow access to the user-selected tab to provide video controls. storage saves the interface language. tabCapture and offscreen allow optional local tab audio processing. Use Reset video to restore original video settings and Stop boost to end audio processing.

Contact and changes

Questions about privacy or exercising applicable data protection rights: Email professionnel de support. The policy will be updated if the extension's data practices change.
